Why Penny

Hundreds of millions of women run real businesses. On paper, they don't exist.

Market vendors, tailors, braiders, cooks. The work is real and the money is real, but it lives in chat threads, cash and mobile money. No books means no proof, and no proof means no loan.

Questions

Good to know

01Do I need a new app?

No. Penny is a contact you add to your existing group texts. Today that's iMessage in the US; WhatsApp, for the rest of the world, is next.

02Which payment apps does she understand?

Any of them. She reads the conversation and payment screenshots, so Cash App, Zelle, Venmo, mobile money and "I'll bring cash" all work.

03What if Penny gets something wrong?

Tap Edit or reply with the right amount. Totals are recalculated with plain math, so the fix shows up everywhere, including the report.

04Is this demo using real people's data?

No. Every name, message and number in this demo is synthetic.
