Questions

Good to know

01Do I need a new app?

No. Penny is a contact you add to your existing group texts. Today that's iMessage, and WhatsApp is next.

02Will my clients know she's there?

Yes. Penny introduces herself once when she's added, so everyone knows. After that she stays quiet and only messages you.

03What if Penny gets something wrong?

Every entry waits for your OK. Reply with the right amount to fix it, or "no" to skip it.

04How does this help me get a loan?

Lenders want proof of steady income. Penny's report shows weekly income, consistency, repeat clients and money owed, all built from transactions you confirmed.
